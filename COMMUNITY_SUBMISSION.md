# Community Contribution submission

- **Kind:** teach
- **Title:** VEC Rule Scenario Trainer — 30 interactive scenarios for data, Agent Team and P3 rules
- **Public link:** https://github.com/random-guy-05/vec-rule-scenario-trainer
- **What it is, and who it helps:** A self-contained, offline quiz that turns subtle Challenge rules into 30 concrete cases. It practices exact and protected external-data boundaries, genotype exclusions, Agent Team locks and evidence, scarce P3 submission attempts, and the Generality Award's shared-architecture test. Each answer links to the official source and explains the reasoning, helping entrants check their understanding before acting.
- **How someone else runs it:** Open `index.html` in a browser. No installation, network connection, or account is needed.
