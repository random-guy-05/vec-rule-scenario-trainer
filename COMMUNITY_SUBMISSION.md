# Community Contribution submission

- **Kind:** expand
- **Title:** VEC Rule Scenario Trainer — 33 cases, with Spanish and Simplified Chinese editions
- **Public link:** https://random-guy-05.github.io/vec-rule-scenario-trainer/
- **Source:** https://github.com/random-guy-05/vec-rule-scenario-trainer
- **What it is, and who it helps:** Adds complete Spanish and Simplified Chinese translations to all 33 cases in the existing offline rule-practice quiz, and adds a three-case module that clarifies when returned scores may guide selection and when using them to recover hidden-target values is prohibited. It helps participants check data-eligibility boundaries, Agent Team autonomy/evidence, phase-specific submission limits, score-use boundaries, and the Generality Award without needing to learn those distinctions from English alone. Each answer preserves the English scenario's correct-answer index and links to the relevant official source. The translations are a community first edition; they are not official Challenge translations and invite native-speaker corrections.
- **How someone else runs it:** Open the hosted page and choose English, Español, or 简体中文. For offline use, download `index.html` and `translations.js` into the same folder and open `index.html`; no installation, account, data file, or network connection is needed after download.
